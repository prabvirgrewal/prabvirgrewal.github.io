About

I got into engineering through robotics. Before I joined a team I was already building things out of metal and wood, and VEX gave me a place to turn that into real machines.

As captain and head builder of VEX Team 1104V, I took a robot that was slow and clunky at our first event and led a full rebuild, all the way to the World Championship. Along the way I learned that most matches are won by planning, testing and attention to detail, not just clever design.

At McMaster I'm studying PLC programming, instrumentation and control, electronics and CAD. Outside class I build Arduino projects, referee VEX IQ for younger students, and help run events for student clubs. My goal is to work on automation and robotics at companies like Tesla and Apple.
